# Consent form: Mind over Chicken playtest

*Read this aloud, answer any questions, and only start once it's signed. Keep signed forms separate from the data: the data sheets use participant codes only.*

## What this is

We're testing a game, not you. You'll play *Mind over Chicken* for about 25 minutes, sometimes saying out loud what you're thinking. Then you'll fill in a short survey and answer a few questions. The whole session takes about 40–45 minutes.

## Your rights

- **Taking part is voluntary.** You can stop at any time, skip any question, or withdraw your data later, without giving a reason.
- **No academic consequences.** Taking part, stopping or withdrawing has no effect on any grade, assessment or relationship with staff.
- **You can't do it wrong.** If something is confusing, that's what we need to know.
